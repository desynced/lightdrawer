Example: for artists

You post a preview, pitch a commission, or enter a contest, and later you need to show the piece was yours, and that you had it by a certain day. You may not want to hand the full file to anyone to do that. LightDrawer lets you stamp work-in-progress and finished pieces and keep the files to yourself. It is free, there is no account, and your art is never uploaded.

Your art is never uploaded. Only a fingerprint leaves your device. It is free, and nobody needs an account.

A fingerprint is a short code calculated from a file. Change even one pixel or one note and the fingerprint changes completely. A LightDrawer receipt records that fingerprint with independent time witnesses, so anyone can check later that a file with that exact fingerprint existed by that time.

This page is about a routine for working artists: illustrators, painters, designers, musicians, and writers. If you just have one finished song, story, or idea to date, the shorter you made it first example is enough.

The idea

  1. You stamp as you go: sketches, milestone saves of your project file, and the finished piece.
  2. You keep the files private. Only fingerprints leave your device.
  3. You can share a fingerprint without the file, for example in a post or an email, as a public marker that you had something by then.
  4. If a question comes up, you show the file and its receipt to the people who need to see it, and nobody else.

A series of dated receipts, from rough sketch to final piece, tells a stronger story than one date at the end.

What this does and does not prove

It does show

  • that a file with this exact fingerprint existed no later than the stamped times, and
  • that the file you show later is the same file, byte for byte, as the one you stamped.

It does not show

  • That you made it. A receipt does not say who created anything. Your process files, sketches, and people who watched you work all help with that.
  • How it was made. It does not show whether a piece was drawn by hand, edited, or generated.
  • That it is original, or that nobody had a similar idea first.
  • Ownership or rights. It does not register a copyright, and it is not a license or a contract.

LightDrawer is extra evidence of timing and integrity. It does not replace copyright registration, a contract with a client, or a lawyer. If a dispute matters, ask a lawyer what evidence counts where you live.

How to do it, step by step

While you work

  1. Pick your milestones. For example: first sketch, line art, color, and final. For music: demo, rough mix, final master.
  2. Save a copy at each milestone. Use your app’s own project format (with layers or tracks) when you can. It shows more of your process than a flat image.
  3. Stamp each milestone copy. Go to lightdrawer.org, drop it in, and download the receipt. Keep working on a new copy, not the stamped one.
  4. Keep each copy next to its receipt, with matching names, for example fox-03-color.psd and fox-03-color-receipt.zip.

Physical work

Photograph the piece at each stage, in good light, with the date written on a card in the frame if you like. Stamp the photos the same day. This dates the photos, not the painting itself, so keep the photos unedited.

When it is finished

  1. Stamp the full-resolution final and the project file.
  2. Check it right away. Open Check a receipt and drop in the receipt and the copy you are keeping. It should match.
  3. Back up everything, files and receipts, somewhere other than your main computer.

Sharing without giving the file away

  • Post a smaller preview online, and keep the full file private.
  • Optional: add the full file’s fingerprint to your post or portfolio page. It reveals nothing about the art, but later you can show the file that matches it.
  • For commissions, stamp each sketch and proof you send, so you have a record of what you sent and when.

If something does not match

What happenedWhat it usually meansWhat to do
Your file matches its receiptIt is the exact file you stamped.Nothing.
Your file no longer matchesYou saved more work into the stamped copy.Look for the untouched copy in your backup. Next time, keep working on a new copy.
A copy from social media does not matchPlatforms resize and re-compress images and audio.This is normal. Show your original and its receipt instead.
An exported image does not match the project fileThey are different files, each with its own fingerprint.Stamp both.

Renaming a file does not change its fingerprint. Saving more work into it does.

Sample note for the receipt

The note is optional and saved inside your receipt, in your own words. Anyone you give the receipt to can read it.

“Fox in Snow,” color stage, layered project file. Drawn by me, Mia Chen. Third of four milestones.

Sample line for a post or portfolio

Full-resolution original stamped with LightDrawer on the day I finished it. Fingerprint: (paste the code from your receipt)

Sample line for a commission or contest entry

I keep dated LightDrawer receipts for my sketches and finished files. I can share them with you on request, and you can check them at lightdrawer.org/verify.html.

Good to know

  • Your art stays private. Only the fingerprint leaves your device. Nobody can rebuild a piece from its fingerprint. Large files are fine; they are read in pieces on your device.
  • Your receipt is not anonymous. It includes the file name and any note. Share receipts only with people who need them.
  • Leave the public log box unchecked unless you want a permanent public entry. It shows only a fingerprint, but it cannot be removed. Posting the fingerprint yourself is an option you control, but the post’s date comes from the platform, not an independent witness.
  • Content Credentials. If your app adds Content Credentials, LightDrawer shows them, but it does not check whether they are valid.
  • Timing. The timestamp authority’s record is in your receipt right away. The Bitcoin and certificate-log records finish over the next few hours. When you check the receipt online after that, the checker can fetch the finished records.
  • A second opinion. Any computer can calculate the same fingerprint without LightDrawer. On Windows, run certutil -hashfile fox.psd SHA256. On Mac or Linux, run shasum -a 256 fox.psd.
  • If LightDrawer disappears, your receipts still work. Each one has its own checker, and the time records live on independent public systems.
