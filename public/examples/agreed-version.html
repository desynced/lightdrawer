Example: the exact version you agreed to

Agreements go through drafts. Later, two people can end up holding two slightly different files, each sure theirs is the one that was signed. LightDrawer gives the final version a fingerprint that both sides can record on the day. If anyone edits the file later, even by one word, the receipt will not match. It is free, nobody needs an account, and the document is never uploaded.

Your agreement is never uploaded. Only a fingerprint leaves your device. It is free, and nobody needs an account.

A fingerprint is a short code calculated from a file. Change even one character and the fingerprint changes completely. A LightDrawer receipt records that fingerprint with independent time witnesses, so anyone can check later that a file with that exact fingerprint existed by that time.

This page is about one agreement between two or more people: a lease, a freelance contract, a loan between friends, a settlement, a group’s rules. If you run a business and want a routine for many contracts, invoices, and designs, see the example for small businesses.

The idea

  1. Everyone agrees on one final file, usually a PDF.
  2. It is stamped on the day it is signed. Ideally each side stamps it, so nobody has to rely on the other’s receipt.
  3. Both sides write down the fingerprint, for example in the email that sends the signed copy.
  4. If there is a question later, anyone can drop a file and a receipt into the checker. A match means it is the agreed version. No match means it is not.

What this does and does not prove

It does show

  • that a file with this exact fingerprint existed no later than the stamped times, and
  • whether a file someone shows later is the same file, byte for byte, as the one that was stamped.

It does not show

  • That anyone agreed to it or signed it. A receipt is not a signature. Keep using real signatures, whether ink or an e-signature service.
  • Who the parties are. It says nothing about identity.
  • That the agreement is fair, valid, or enforceable. That depends on what it says and the law where you are.
  • What was said outside the document. It only covers the file.

LightDrawer is extra evidence of timing and integrity. It does not replace a lawyer, a notary, a witness, or any signing or filing step your agreement needs. For anything important, have a lawyer review the agreement itself.

How to do it, step by step

Before signing

  1. Settle on the final draft. Turn off tracked changes and remove comments.
  2. Make a PDF. Everyone should sign the same PDF, not separate copies made from a word processor. Two people exporting the “same” document will usually get files with different fingerprints.
  3. Optional: stamp the final unsigned PDF and share the fingerprint, so everyone can confirm they are about to sign the same file.

On the day it is signed

  1. Get the final signed file. If you use an e-signature service, download the completed PDF once and keep that download. Some services produce a new file each time you download, and a new file can have a different fingerprint.
  2. Stamp it. Go to lightdrawer.org, drop in the signed PDF, add a short note if you like (sample below), and download the receipt.
  3. Check it right away. Open Check a receipt and drop in the receipt and the copy you are keeping. It should match.
  4. Share the fingerprint. Send the other side a short message with the fingerprint (sample below). Ask them to stamp their copy too. If both fingerprints are the same, you both hold the identical file.
  5. Keep the file and receipt together, and back them up somewhere else.

Signed on paper?

Scan the signed pages once into a PDF and stamp that scan. Be clear about what this covers: it shows the scan has not changed since you stamped it. It does not cover the paper itself, so keep the paper original safe too. A new scan of the same paper will have a different fingerprint, so share the scan you stamped.

If the agreement changes later

Do not edit the stamped file. Put the change in a new file, such as an amendment, have it signed, and stamp that too. Keep every version with its own receipt.

If something does not match

What happenedWhat it usually meansWhat to do
Your file matches your receiptIt is the exact file you stamped.Nothing.
Your fingerprint and theirs differ on the dayYou are holding different files, perhaps two downloads or two exports.Stop and sort it out now. Agree on one file and both stamp that one.
A file shown later does not match the receiptIt is not the stamped file. It may have been edited, re-saved, or re-exported.Compare it with the copy you stamped. Show both, with your receipt.
Your own copy does not matchYou may have opened and saved it, or kept a different download.Look in your email or backup for the copy you stamped.

Renaming a file does not change its fingerprint. Opening and saving it again, or “printing to PDF,” usually does.

Sample note for the receipt

The note is optional and saved inside your receipt, in your own words. Anyone you give the receipt to can read it.

Signed services agreement between Taylor Design and Northside Bakery, final version, signed by both on June 10. This is the completed PDF downloaded from our e-signature service.

Sample line for the email that sends the signed copy

Attached is the signed agreement. I stamped it with LightDrawer today. Its fingerprint is below. Could you stamp your copy too and check that the fingerprint matches? Anyone can check a receipt at lightdrawer.org/verify.html.

Good to know

  • The document stays private. Only the fingerprint leaves your device. Nobody can rebuild a contract from its fingerprint.
  • Your note travels with the receipt. Do not put account numbers or other private details in it.
  • Leave the public log box unchecked unless both sides want a permanent public entry. It shows only a fingerprint, but it cannot be removed.
  • Timing. The timestamp authority’s record is in your receipt right away. The Bitcoin and certificate-log records finish over the next few hours. When you check the receipt online after that, the checker can fetch the finished records.
  • A second opinion. Any computer can calculate the same fingerprint without LightDrawer. On Windows, run certutil -hashfile agreement.pdf SHA256. On Mac or Linux, run shasum -a 256 agreement.pdf. Both sides can compare this way too.
  • If LightDrawer disappears, your receipts still work. Each one has its own checker, and the time records live on independent public systems.
