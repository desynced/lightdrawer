Example: for small businesses

A client says the invoice you sent last spring had a different total. A former partner says the logo was their idea. A supplier sends “the same” contract back with one clause changed. Small businesses often need to point back to an exact file from months ago. LightDrawer gives each important file a dated receipt that anyone can check. It is free, there is no account, and your files are never uploaded.

Your files are never uploaded. Only a fingerprint leaves your device. It is free, and nobody needs an account.

A fingerprint is a short code calculated from a file. Change even one character and the fingerprint changes completely. A LightDrawer receipt records that fingerprint with independent time witnesses, so anyone can check later that a file with that exact fingerprint existed by that time.

This page is about building a simple routine for a business. For one agreement between two parties, see the exact version you agreed to, which covers signing day in more detail.

The idea

  1. You pick the files that matter: signed contracts, quotes, invoices, design proofs, and final deliverables.
  2. You stamp each one when it is final, the day it is signed, sent, or delivered.
  3. You keep each file next to its receipt, in a folder you already back up.
  4. If a question comes up, you show the file and its receipt. The other side can check them without trusting you or LightDrawer.

What this does and does not prove

It does show

  • that a file with this exact fingerprint existed no later than the stamped times, and
  • that the file you show later is the same file, byte for byte, as the one you stamped.

It does not show

  • That a file was sent, received, read, or paid. Keep your email records, delivery confirmations, and payment records.
  • That anyone agreed to it. A receipt is not a signature.
  • Who created a design or idea. It shows when you had a file, not who made it.
  • That the numbers or content are correct.

LightDrawer is extra evidence of timing and integrity. It is not a certification, and it does not make your records meet any tax, accounting, or invoicing rule. It does not replace your accountant, your lawyer, or your bookkeeping system. Ask them what records you are required to keep.

How to do it, step by step

Set it up once

  1. Choose what to stamp. A good start: signed contracts, quotes the client accepted, invoices, and final design files. Skip everyday drafts.
  2. Make a receipts habit in your folders. Keep each receipt next to its file with a matching name, for example INV-1042.pdf and INV-1042-receipt.zip.
  3. Make a simple log, if you like. A spreadsheet with: date, client, file name, fingerprint, and what it is. It makes things easy to find later.
  4. Check your backup covers the folder. A receipt only helps if you still have the exact file.

Contracts and quotes

Stamp the final signed PDF on the day it is signed, and send the fingerprint to the other side. The agreed-version example walks through this.

Invoices

  1. Export the invoice as a PDF from your invoicing tool.
  2. Stamp that PDF before or right after you send it, and send that same file. If your tool makes a new PDF each time you download, keep the one you stamped.
  3. Optional: put the fingerprint in the email, so the client can check the file they received.

Designs and deliverables

  1. Stamp each proof or concept when you send it to a client, and the final files when you deliver them.
  2. For many files, put a delivery in one ZIP and stamp the ZIP. Keep that exact ZIP.
  3. Stamp early concepts too. If a client later says they came up with an idea, a series of dated receipts helps show how the work developed.

Check each receipt once

After stamping, open Check a receipt and drop in the receipt and the copy you are keeping. It should match. This takes a few seconds and catches mix-ups early.

If something does not match

What happenedWhat it usually meansWhat to do
Your file matches its receiptIt is the exact file you stamped.Nothing.
A file someone sends back does not matchIt is not the file you stamped. It may have been edited or re-exported.Show your stamped copy and its receipt. Point out what differs.
Your own copy does not matchIt was re-exported, opened and saved, or it is a different download.Look in your sent email or backup for the stamped copy.
A ZIP does not matchIt was made again from the same files.Use the original ZIP.

Renaming a file does not change its fingerprint. Opening and saving it again usually does.

Sample note for the receipt

The note is optional and saved inside your receipt, in your own words. Anyone you give the receipt to can read it.

Invoice INV-1042 to Northside Bakery, as sent on April 2.

Do not put bank details, prices you want kept private, or customer personal information in the note.

Sample line for an invoice email

Invoice INV-1042 is attached. Its LightDrawer fingerprint is below, so you can check this is the exact file I sent at lightdrawer.org/verify.html.

Fingerprint: (paste the code from your receipt)

Sample line for a contract or terms page

Final versions of this agreement and its deliverables are stamped with LightDrawer. Either party may check a file against its receipt at lightdrawer.org/verify.html.

Have your lawyer look at any wording you add to a contract.

Good to know

  • Your files stay private. Only the fingerprint leaves your computer. Nobody can rebuild a contract or design from its fingerprint.
  • Leave the public log box unchecked unless you want a permanent public entry. It shows only a fingerprint, but it cannot be removed.
  • Timing. The timestamp authority’s record is in your receipt right away. The Bitcoin and certificate-log records finish over the next few hours. When you check the receipt online after that, the checker can fetch the finished records.
  • Want customers to stamp files they send you? You can add a free “Stamp this with LightDrawer” button to your website. See the button guide.
  • A second opinion. Any computer can calculate the same fingerprint without LightDrawer. On Windows, run certutil -hashfile INV-1042.pdf SHA256. On Mac or Linux, run shasum -a 256 INV-1042.pdf.
  • If LightDrawer disappears, your receipts still work. Each one has its own checker, and the time records live on independent public systems.
